/questions

Things owners ask me.

Is the demo really free?

Yes. I build it first so you can see exactly what you'd get. If it's not for you, that's the end of it. No contract, no bill.

Who owns the website?

You do. Your domain is registered in your name, and if you ever want to move the site somewhere else, I'll hand over everything.

What else will I pay for?

Just your domain name, which usually runs about $10 to $20 a year. Hosting and updates are covered by the $40 monthly plan.

How long does it take?

Most one-page sites are live about a week after you approve the demo. Each extra page adds about a day.

Can I make changes myself?

You can, but you don't have to. Send me a text or email with the change and I'll take care of it as part of the monthly plan.

Do you only work in Vancouver?

No. I'm based in Vancouver, Washington, but I build sites for businesses anywhere. We can go over your demo by phone or video call, and everything else happens online.
